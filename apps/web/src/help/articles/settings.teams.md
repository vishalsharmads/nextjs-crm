Group people so the "team" record scope works: people with team scope see records owned by anyone in their teams.

Add or remove members, and rename or delete teams. A person can be in several teams.

Because a team widens what its members can see, only an admin can add you to a team you are not already in. People who manage users can still change other people's teams.
