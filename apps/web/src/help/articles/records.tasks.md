Your to-do list: tasks, calls and meetings, grouped into **Overdue**, **Today**, **Upcoming** and **No date**. **Done** lists what was completed, newest first.

- **Add a task** in the bar at the top (press `c` from anywhere on the page). Give it a due date there or later. Tasks added from a record stay linked to that record.
- **Tick** the round box to complete a task; the toast lets you undo. Completing a repeating task creates its next occurrence.
- **Reschedule** from a task's ⋯ menu: tomorrow, next Monday, a date you pick, or no date. Undo puts the old date back.
- **Select** several tasks with the square boxes to complete them or move them to tomorrow or next week in one go.
- **Filter** by type and priority. If you can see other people's tasks, switch between your own and your team's (or everyone's).
- **Keyboard**: `j` and `k` move through the list, `x` completes or reopens, `Enter` opens the task.
- Open a task to edit it, add a checklist, set a reminder or repeat, and discuss it in comments.
