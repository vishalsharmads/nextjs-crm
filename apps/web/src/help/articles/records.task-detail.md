One task, call or meeting on its own page.

- Change the subject, type, due date, priority or assignee in place. Each change saves on its own and appears in the history.
- Write a longer description and save it.
- **Complete** marks it done; **Reopen** brings it back to your list.
- The record it belongs to is linked on the left.

## Comments

- Discuss the work under **Comments**. The assignee and the person who created it are told about new comments.
- **Mention someone** to notify them by name. Only people who can already see the task are notified; others are left out silently.
- You can edit or delete your own comments. Deleted comments are removed, not hidden.
