Create record types your industry needs that are not built in, such as vehicles, properties or equipment.

1. Choose **New object** and give it a singular and plural name. The key is made from the name and cannot change later.
2. Choose an icon and what each record links to: {{contactPluralLower}}, {{organizationPluralLower}}, {{dealPluralLower}}, {{leadPluralLower}} or other objects. Links can be added later but not removed.
3. Add fields in **Custom fields**, then arrange them in **Layouts**.
4. Give roles the new object's permissions in **Roles**.

You can have up to 20 objects. Archiving hides an object and its records; nothing is deleted, and restoring brings them back.
