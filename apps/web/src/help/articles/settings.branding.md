Your name, logo, colors and fonts, shown everywhere: the app, emails, documents and the client portal.

- **Name and logo**: replace the app name and the logo in the sidebar, emails and printed documents.
- **Colors**: pick the main and accent colors for light and dark mode. Text colors are checked for readability.
- **Font and corners**: choose a font and how round buttons and panels are.
- **Sign-in page**: the title and message people see before they sign in.
