Change your name, notifications, password and two-factor authentication, see where you are signed in, and manage access tokens and connected AI apps.

- **Notifications**: choose whether notifications also arrive by email, and turn on a daily digest: one email each morning (from 08:00 in the workspace time zone) with your tasks due today and overdue. When the workspace uses minimal notification emails, the digest is not sent.
- **Password**: changing it signs you out everywhere else and disconnects your AI apps and tokens.
- **Two-factor authentication**: scan the code with an authenticator app, then enter a code to confirm. Keep the recovery codes somewhere safe.
- **Active sessions**: sign out a device you no longer use.
- **Access tokens**: for AI clients that connect over MCP. A read-only token can only look; a full token can also make changes, always with your permissions.
- **Connected apps**: AI apps you approved by signing in. Disconnect them here.
