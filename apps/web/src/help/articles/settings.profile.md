Change your name, notifications, password and two-factor authentication, see where you are signed in, and manage access tokens and connected AI apps.

- **Notifications**: choose whether notifications also arrive by email, and turn on a daily digest: one email each morning (from 08:00 in the workspace time zone) with your tasks due today and overdue. When the workspace uses minimal notification emails, the digest is not sent.
- **By kind**: for each kind of notification (assigned to me, mentioned me, comments on my records, reminders, shared with me, everything else) choose email and push separately. Notifications always appear under the bell.
- **Push on this device**: turn it on in each browser or installed app where you want pop-up notifications, even when {{appName}} is closed. Your browser asks for permission first. Under the health-data profile, push messages only say that something new arrived. Signing out everywhere or changing your password turns push off on every device.
- **Install the app**: in a supported browser, use its **Install** or **Add to Home Screen** option. The installed app keeps no customer data on the device; without a connection it shows an offline page.
- **Password**: changing it signs you out everywhere else and disconnects your AI apps and tokens.
- **Two-factor authentication**: scan the code with an authenticator app, then enter a code to confirm. Keep the recovery codes somewhere safe.
- **Active sessions**: sign out a device you no longer use.
- **Access tokens**: for AI clients that connect over MCP. A read-only token can only look; a full token can also make changes, always with your permissions.
- **Connected apps**: AI apps you approved by signing in. Disconnect them here.

## Calendar

- **Your calendar**: connect one calendar by its CalDAV address (most calendar services and servers offer one; use an app password if asked). Every 15 minutes, events whose guests include {{contactPluralLower}} or {{leadPluralLower}} you can see become meetings on their records, meetings you add here appear in that calendar, and changes to the time or title go both ways (the most recent change wins). Repeating events only count as busy time.
- **Subscribe from another calendar**: create a private address of your meetings and tasks with a time (titles and times only, never notes), for calendar apps that subscribe to a web address. Anyone with the address can see the titles and times, so replace or remove it if it is shared by mistake.
