What each role can do (permissions) and whose records it sees (scope: own, team or all).

- **Permissions** are grouped by area. A role can only give permissions its editor has.
- **Scope** per record type: **own** (records you own or are assigned), **team** (records of anyone in your teams) or **all**.
- **Sharing** adds single records on top of scope: someone with own scope sees a colleague's record once it is shared with them or their team. A share never adds a permission the role lacks. **Share** lets a role share records in its scope (owners can always share their own).
- The **Admin** role can do everything and cannot be changed.
