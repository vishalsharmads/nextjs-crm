Custom objects are record types your admin created for things your work tracks, such as vehicles, properties or equipment. They work like the other lists: create, search, filter, views, bulk actions, import, export, history and the trash.

Each object has a name, an owner, links to other records and any custom fields your admin added. What you can see and do depends on your role's permissions for that object.

Records of these types have a Comments tab too: mention a colleague to notify them, if they can open the record. Owners and admins can also share a record with one more person or team (⋯ → **Share**).
