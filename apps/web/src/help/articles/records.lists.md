Every list (records, objects and module records) works the same way.

- **Columns**: choose and reorder columns from the column menu.
- **Filters**: combine conditions on any filterable field, such as owner, stage, dates or custom fields.
- **Views**: save the current filters, sort and columns as a tab. Views are yours unless you share them (needs permission).
- **Keyboard**: `j` and `k` move between rows, `Enter` opens, `x` selects.
- **Export**: download the filtered list as a spreadsheet (needs the export permission).
