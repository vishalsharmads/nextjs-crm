Reports cover the {{dealPluralLower}} you are allowed to see.

## Forecast

Open value by expected close month, with the value **weighted** by each stage's probability, and what was won each month. Overdue close dates and {{dealPluralLower}} with no date are counted separately.

## Quotas

A monthly target per person and how much of it has been won (attainment). Managers with the quota permission set targets right in the table.

## Velocity

Win rate, average won value, average days to win, and **velocity**: the value you can expect to win per day. Below it, the average time {{dealPluralLower}} spend in each stage.

## Custom

Build your own report: pick a record type, group it by a field (optionally broken down by a second field), and count, total or average a number. Save it, share it, and add it to your home dashboard.

## Currencies

Reports use the workspace currency. Turn on **All currencies** to convert other currencies with the rates in Settings → Currencies; currencies without a rate are listed and left out.
