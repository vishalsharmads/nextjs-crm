The {{dealPluralLower}} list shows everyone your role lets you see. Open one to see its details, history and everything linked to it.

## What you can do

- **Create**: the **New {{deal}}** button, or press `c`. Required fields are marked.
- **Find**: search, filter by any field, sort by a column, and save the result as a view tab so you can come back to it.
- **Peek or open**: click a row to peek at it on the side; open the full page with its link or `Enter`.
- **Edit**: change a field in place on the record page. You can undo from the message that appears.
- **Work in bulk**: tick several rows to assign an owner, change a field, add tags or delete them.
- **Log what happens**: add notes, tasks, calls and meetings from the record page. They appear in its timeline.
- **Board**: drag a card to another stage, pick **Move to** from the ⋯ on the card, or use the stage track on its page. Moving to a won or lost stage closes it; marking one lost asks for a reason.
- **Value and close date** feed the pipeline widget, the forecast and quotas (Reports).
- **Line items** (with the Products module): list what you are selling; the total can become the value.

## Fields

Besides the built-in fields, your admin can add custom fields and arrange them (Settings → Custom fields and Layouts). Some fields may be read-only or hidden for your role. You can link a {{dealLower}} only to {{contactPluralLower}} and {{organizationPluralLower}} you can see; a link someone else made stays when you edit the {{dealLower}}.
