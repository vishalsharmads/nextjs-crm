Track access and erasure requests, restrict processing, and export or erase a person’s data.

- **Requests**: log a request and its deadline (set in Compliance).
- **Export**: a download of everything held about a person.
- **Anonymize or delete**: removes the person's data everywhere it was copied, including emails, webhook payloads and assistant conversations.
- **Restrict**: keep the record but stop automations, webhooks and emails from using it.
