Meetings, calls and tasks with a date, by day, week or month. Times are shown in your workspace time zone.

- **Click** an item to open it.
- **Drag** an item to move it; drag the bottom edge of a meeting to change how long it lasts. The toast lets you undo.
- **Click or drag across empty time** to plan a meeting, call or task in that slot.
- **Only mine** shows what is assigned to you; turn it off to see everything you have access to.
