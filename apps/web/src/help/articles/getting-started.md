{{appName}} keeps everything about the {{contactPluralLower}} and {{organizationPluralLower}} you work with in one place: who they are, what you are working on with them, and what happens next.

## Find your way

- **Sidebar**: the main lists ({{contactPlural}}, {{organizationPlural}}, {{dealPlural}}, tasks, calendar) and the modules your workspace uses.
- **Search** (`⌘K` or `Ctrl K`, or `/`): jump to any record, page or action. Type a name, an email, a number or words from a note; **See all results** opens the full search page.
- **Help** (`?`): the ⓘ button at the top of every page explains that page. This knowledge base collects every article.
- **Your menu** (your picture, top right): your profile, theme, keyboard shortcuts and sign out.

## A typical day

1. Open **Home** to see your pipeline, tasks due and recent activity. Choose **Edit dashboard** to add, resize or remove widgets, including saved reports. Widgets follow your access: they count only the records you can see, and a widget built on a field hidden from your role shows as unavailable.
2. Work through your **Tasks**: call, email, meet, then mark them done.
3. Move {{dealPluralLower}} forward on the board as they progress.
4. Log notes on the record after every conversation, so your team knows what happened.

## Good to know

- You see the records your role allows: your own, your team's or everyone's.
- Changes are kept in each record's history, and an admin can see the audit log.
- Deleted records go to the trash first and can be restored.
- Settings pages ask before you leave with changes you have not saved. Actions that are hard to undo, such as deleting a draft or voiding a document, ask you to confirm first.
- Admins setting up a new workspace can use **Set up with AI** on the assistant page (when the assistant module is on): it asks about the business, proposes a setup to approve, and helps import existing spreadsheets.
