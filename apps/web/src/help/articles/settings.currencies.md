Exchange rates that reports use to convert other currencies into the workspace currency.

Enter how much one unit of each currency is worth in the workspace currency, for example 1 EUR = 1.08 USD. Records keep their own currency; only reports convert, and only when you turn conversion on. Currencies with no rate are listed above the report and left out.
