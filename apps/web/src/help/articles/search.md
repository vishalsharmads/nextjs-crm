Find any record you can open, by name, email, phone number or reference, and the text of notes, emails, calls, meetings and tasks.

- Press `⌘K` (`Ctrl K`) anywhere and type. The best matches appear as you pause typing; choose **See all results** for this page.
- Results are grouped by record type and ranked: an exact name first, then names that start with what you typed, then other matches.
- Text custom fields (text, email, phone, link) marked **Filterable** are searched too, unless the field is hidden from you or holds sensitive data. The field's name shows next to a match found there.
- A note, email, call or meeting opens the {{contactLower}}, {{organizationLower}}, {{dealLower}} or {{leadLower}} it belongs to; a task opens the task.
- Search shows only records you may see, the same as your lists.
