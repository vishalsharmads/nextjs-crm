Invite people, give them roles and teams, and deactivate accounts.

- **Invite**: enter an email and choose roles. The person gets an email to set a password.
- **Deactivate**: the person can no longer sign in. Transfer their records to someone else first.
- **Roles** decide what someone can do; **teams** decide whose records "team" scope covers.

Only admins can change other admins.
