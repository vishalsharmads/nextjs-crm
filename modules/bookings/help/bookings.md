Book desks, rooms and equipment by the hour, with clash checks and opening hours.

## What you can do

- **Book** desks, rooms and equipment by the hour on the board. Clashes and closed hours are refused.
- **Change or cancel** a booking by opening it.
- **Resources**: add what can be booked, with its opening hours.

Times are shown in the workspace time zone, so everyone sees the same schedule.
