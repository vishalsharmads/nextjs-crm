Track equipment, keys and other assets lent to people, and stock levels of consumables with reorder alerts.

## What you can do

- **Assets**: register equipment, keys and other items, lend them to people and record when they come back. Each asset keeps its history.
- **Stock**: track quantities of consumables, record what is used or received, and get an alert when stock falls below its reorder level.
- **Search and filter** by status, location or who has an item.
