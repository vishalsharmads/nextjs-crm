Set up {{appName}} around how your business works. The assistant learns about your business, asks a few questions, writes a brief for you to check, and proposes a setup you choose from. Only admins can use it, and nothing changes until you approve it.

## Steps

1. **Your business**: the business name is all that is required, but the more you share (what you sell, who your customers are, your process, your team, what goes wrong today), the better the setup fits. Add your website and choose **Read website**: the assistant reads your home page once and shows the name, description, logo and brand colours it found.
2. **Questions**: the assistant asks one question at a time and says why it asks. Click the suggestions that apply (several when it allows), add your own words, or skip. Choose **That's enough, write the brief** at any point.
3. **Brief**: everything you said, as the assistant understood it: what you offer, your customers, your process stages, what you need to record, your team, the words you use for {{contactPluralLower}} and other records, reports and routines. Correct anything, then **Approve the brief**. **Write it again** asks the assistant for a fresh one.
4. **Setup**: from the approved brief, the assistant proposes a setup for you to choose from.

Your progress is saved: come back to this page to continue, or choose **Start over**.
