A catalog of what you sell, with prices and tax rates, used on quotes and invoices.

## What you can do

- **Catalog**: what you sell, with price, unit and tax rate. Archive products you no longer sell.
- **Use them** on quotes, invoices and as **line items** on {{dealPluralLower}} (the Line items tab on the record page), where the total can become the {{dealLower}} value.
