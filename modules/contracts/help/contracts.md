Agreements with start and end dates, renewal reminders and automatic renewal.

## What you can do

- **Create** a contract with a start and end date, linked to {{contactPluralLower}}, {{organizationPluralLower}} or {{dealPluralLower}}.
- **Renewals**: its owner is reminded before it ends (the notice period is in Settings → Sales documents). Contracts set to renew automatically roll over.
- **Print or save as PDF**, and share it through the client portal.
