A support desk: tickets with priorities, response targets, internal notes and customer replies.

## What you can do

- **Tickets** with a priority, status and owner. Response targets show what needs an answer first.
- **Reply** to the customer, or add **internal notes** only your team sees.
- **Resolve** a ticket when it is done; it can be reopened.
